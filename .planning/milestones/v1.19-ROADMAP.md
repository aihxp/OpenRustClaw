**Goal:** Move the remaining `/control/config`, `/control/config/validate`, and `/control/config/update` orchestration out of `start.rs` so the control-plane config path becomes another bounded application-owned route family.

**Success criteria:**

- `start.rs` becomes the HTTP adapter for config validation and mutation instead of owning the business rules directly
- verification proves the shipped control config contract remains truthful
