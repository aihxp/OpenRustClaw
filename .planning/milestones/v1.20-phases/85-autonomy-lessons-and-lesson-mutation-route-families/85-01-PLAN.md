# Plan 85-01: Extract the Autonomy Lesson-Control Route Family

## Objective

Move the autonomy lesson listing and mutation orchestration out of `start.rs` and behind one shared application-layer lesson-control service.

## Steps

1. Add an autonomy lesson-control service to `openrustclaw-app` for summary shaping, lesson listing, and post-mutation report composition.
2. Replace the inline autonomy lesson handlers in `start.rs` with adapters into that service.
3. Verify the shipped lesson-control route family still preserves the current contract.
