# Plan 83-01: Extract the Channel Registry Lifecycle Route Family

## Objective

Move channel registry account and binding mutation orchestration out of `start.rs` and behind one shared application-layer lifecycle service.

## Steps

1. Add a channel registry lifecycle service to `openrustclaw-app` for account and binding mutation orchestration, bind default shaping, and result composition.
2. Replace the inline channel registry mutation helpers in `start.rs` with adapters into that service.
3. Verify the shipped channel registry mutation route family still preserves the current contract.
