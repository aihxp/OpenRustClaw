# Plan 162-01: Failure Ownership and Evidence Triage

## Goal

Classify the E2E results by ownership and document clearly whether any repair work is actually required.

## Steps

1. Review the E2E and integration results for any failing assertions, crashes, or blocked paths.
2. Classify any blocking issues by ownership across app-lane, native delivery, infrastructure, and bounded legacy exceptions.
3. Record any non-blocking warnings separately so the milestone does not confuse cleanup debt with product breakage.
