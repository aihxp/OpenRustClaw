# Plan 20-01: Add A Typed Enterprise Governance Contract

## Goal

Extend enterprise access with an explicit governance policy and typed governance summary for higher-risk scopes.

## Must Have

- the enterprise access manifest stores governance rules beside the operator registry
- governance rules can express requester roles, approver roles, approval mode, and separation-of-duties expectations
- enterprise access and enterprise admin summaries expose the governance contract through typed Rust reports

## Expected Files

- `crates/cli/src/commands/enterprise_access.rs`
- `crates/cli/src/commands/inspect.rs`
- `crates/cli/src/commands/start.rs`

## Verification

- targeted tests for governance defaults, updates, and typed summary coverage
