# Phase 17 Plan 02: Export enterprise policy and audit evidence

## Objective
- **What:** Add a durable enterprise audit export bundle.
- **Why:** Operators need to preserve the current policy snapshot together with recent enterprise evidence instead of manually collecting several raw files.
- **Output:** A shipped enterprise audit export route and file-backed JSON bundle.
