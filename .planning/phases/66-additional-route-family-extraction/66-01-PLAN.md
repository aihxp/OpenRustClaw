# Plan 66-01: Extract the Enterprise Access Route Family

## Objective

Move the enterprise access write handlers out of inline `start.rs` orchestration and behind a greenfield application-layer control service.

## Steps

1. Add an enterprise access control service to `openrustclaw-app`.
2. Replace the inline bootstrap, operator-upsert, and governance-rule-upsert orchestration in `start.rs` with an adapter into that service.
3. Preserve the current enterprise access route contract through focused regression tests.
