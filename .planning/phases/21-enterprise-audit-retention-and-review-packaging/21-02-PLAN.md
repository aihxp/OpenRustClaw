# Plan 21-02: Package Governance And Autonomy Evidence For Review

## Goal

Make enterprise audit exports and review summaries carry richer governance and supervised-autonomy evidence than the current raw bundle.

- enterprise audit bundle includes governance and supervision context alongside policy and recent operator history

- `crates/cli/src/commands/inspect.rs`
- `crates/cli/src/commands/start.rs`

## Verification

- targeted tests for richer audit bundle contents and typed review-summary behavior
